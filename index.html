AI, hands-on
AI turns an idea into a working prototype — concrete enough for specialists to build from.

Two modes, both essential: prototyping with AI to move quickly, and shaping products around AI that hold up in production.

01 · Building with AI

Making an idea tangible, fast

Claude and Claude Code turn a concept into something testable before the spec is finished. Solar Ledger is the proof of range: a device-mesh app (Tauri + SQLite) that syncs across phone, laptop and tablet with no central server, shaped end to end as a working prototype — architecture, a front end, even packaged binaries — enough to prove the idea is feasible. Building it for production is a specialist's job; making it concrete enough to hand over is the point.

02 · Building around AI

AI as product material, shaped to the job

General LLM APIs are expensive and slow at cohort scale. A small-model migration is the case in point: fine-tuning small models with LoRA (Qwen, TinyLlama on Ollama) to prove a self-hosted model could carry the load, then handing the production pipeline to engineering. Every AI verdict stays defensible: grounded, evaluated, and traceable to something a human can check.

How I work

Turning problems into products.

A few principles behind the work, and the method that runs underneath.

01

Defensible by design

Human in the loop, and auditable. If a stakeholder can't see why the system decided something, they won't stake a decision on it.

02

One blueprint, many outputs

Build the blueprint once and reuse it, rather than reinventing the wheel for every new output.

03

Expertise is the foundation

Grounded in real expertise — whether owned, learned, or brought in for the job.

04

Built for adoption

The end the work aims at: an easy hop onto a trusted, fire-and-forget tool that quietly does exactly what's expected of it.

Instrumentation

Features that report back.

Flagship assessment · usage tracker

Turn a black box into decisions

The flagship assessment had no single place to see who used what. A usage-tracking layer now makes every report traceable, from the first ever generated to today: which client deployed how many, whether a user has been assessed before and where that report is, how far they are, and their scores. The team helps clients with facts, not guesses.

Platform · unified error page

Make failure debuggable

Error pages used to show a code and nothing else. A unified error page now captures the page, API, browser, device and timestamp of each issue, so a user can screenshot and send it to support instead of anyone digging through logs.
